## Purpose

Capture the ErosOS surface strategy for ordinary users across mobile, smart-glasses, spatial-computing, vehicle, home, and future embodied interfaces.

The core product is not a single device or screen. The product is continuity with governed intelligence across the surfaces of a person's life.

This is roadmap and product-architecture planning only. It does not authorize hardware development, device manufacturing, platform integration, public compatibility claims, runtime changes, direct provider access, CI changes, repo extraction, or protected Brain modifications.

## Strategic premise

ErosOS should not be designed only for developers, AI power users, or conventional desktop-computer users.

Its primary market includes ordinary people who may:

- use only a phone or tablet;
- rarely use a traditional computer;
- avoid terminals, APIs, configuration, and model selection;
- want help with daily life, work, family, accessibility, communication, navigation, understanding documents, reminders, and decisions;
- need advanced AI coordination without becoming AI operators.


Every surface must preserve:

- the same ethical geometry and approval boundaries;
- the same user-controlled memory fabric;
- the same capability permissions;
- the same trace, evidence, and uncertainty model;
- continuity of user and project context.

## Surface roles

### Mobile

The primary mass-market surface for users who do not rely on desktop computers.

Expected role:

- natural conversation;
- voice and text;
- daily tasks and reminders;
- approvals;
- messages, appointments, documents, and personal context;
- accessibility-first interaction;
- seamless continuity with desktop and other surfaces.

### Smart glasses

An ambient, hands-free interaction surface for situational assistance.

Potential roles:

- reading and explaining signs, labels, letters, and documents;
- navigation and translation;
- reminders connected to surroundings;
- accessibility assistance;
- saving observations for later review;
- bounded, explicit visual context requests.

Glasses must not become an always-recording surveillance system.

### Spatial computing / XR

An immersive workspace surface for complex cognition and collaboration.

Potential roles:

- spatial project workspaces;
- embodied memory and evidence maps;
- DevForge workspaces;
- simulation and robotics interfaces;
- multi-application cognition environments;
- visible intent, approval, trace, and uncertainty objects.

### Vehicle and home surfaces

Future ambient interfaces for contextual assistance, subject to strict safety, privacy, consent, and platform constraints.

### Future embodiment

Robotics or other embodied systems may become ErosOS surfaces only through separately reviewed capability and safety contracts.

## Product principle

> The device is not the cognition authority.

Hardware, operating systems, and external assistants are interfaces or capabilities. Cogni-Core remains the sole authority for cognition, ethics, memory use, capability selection, and action review.

Changing devices must not replace Eros identity, context, memory, or governance.

## Privacy and consent requirements

Wearable and ambient surfaces require stricter controls than desktop applications.

Any future sensory-device contract must include:

- explicit sensing modes;
- visible capture indicators;
- local processing by default where feasible;
- data minimization;
- bystander-sensitive policies;
- separate permission for sensing, remembering, and acting;
- automatic redaction where appropriate;
- revocable memory;
- user-visible records of retained sensory data;
- fail-closed behavior when consent or authority is unclear;
- quarantine and revocation for compromised capabilities.

## Business and market implications

The target market includes users who do not code and may not use conventional computers regularly.

Future research and business plans should measure:

- mobile-only and mobile-primary user segments;
- barriers to current AI adoption;
- comfort with traditional computer interfaces;
- voice-first and accessibility needs;
- willingness to use one persistent cognition environment across devices;
- trust in wearable and ambient sensing;
- value of continuity across phone, desktop, glasses, and spatial surfaces;
- successful task completion without model selection or technical configuration;
- retention attributable to remembered context and cross-device continuity.

## Recommended sequence

1. Restore and verify the ErosOS desktop/application baseline.
2. Define a product-neutral mobile/desktop cognition client contract.
3. Establish identity, session, memory, permission, evidence, and continuity schemas.
4. Build a mobile-first ErosOS surface.
5. Define a bounded sensory-device contract.
6. Prototype against existing smart-glasses and XR platforms where separately authorized.
7. Consider strategic hardware partnerships only after product and trust validation.
8. Avoid original hardware manufacturing until demand, capital, compliance, and support capacity justify it.
