## Objective

Formalize a developmental architecture for Eros based on seven broad neurodevelopmental processes:

1. neurogenesis;
2. migration;
3. differentiation;
4. maturation;
5. synaptogenesis;
6. pruning;
7. myelogenesis.

These stages are to be used as a systems-development framework, not as a literal claim that software develops identically to a biological brain.

## Core Principle

> Growth is not the accumulation of more cognition. Growth is the progressive organization, refinement, stabilization, and optimization of cognition under ethical governance.

A concise lifecycle:

`generate -> place -> specialize -> stabilize -> integrate -> prune -> optimize`

## Architectural Mapping

| Biological process | Eros equivalent | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Neurogenesis | Creation of a new bounded cognitive component | A mentor, memory function, regulatory process, capability contract, reasoning primitive, sensor, or actuator is introduced in an inert or sandboxed state |
| Migration | Anatomically correct placement | The component is assigned to Neocortex, Limbic, Subcortical, Eros, or Applications according to responsibility |
| Differentiation | Role and authority specialization | Interfaces, permissions, invariants, prohibited behaviors, and superseding authorities are defined |
| Maturation | Validation and stabilization | Tests, failure behavior, interpretability, uncertainty handling, security, and causal tracing are established |
| Synaptogenesis | Governed integration | The component is connected through the canonical signal and cognition path without bypassing Brain authority |
| Pruning | Removal of duplicate, stale, unsafe, or semantically misplaced structures | Shadow ontologies, direct model paths, duplicate executors, stale governance, and redundant routes are removed or quarantined |
| Myelogenesis | Earned optimization | Only validated pathways may receive caching, compilation, indexing, learned routing weights, hardware acceleration, or other performance shortcuts |

## Relationship to Existing Developmental Phases

The existing global progression remains authoritative:

`DEPENDENT -> GUIDED -> SEMI_AUTONOMOUS -> AUTONOMOUS`

The seven-stage lifecycle operates beneath that progression at the component level.

A mature global system must not automatically treat every newly added component as mature. Each component must complete its own bounded developmental cycle and remain subordinate to the global phase and Brain authority.

## Proposed Developmental Dimensions

### 1. Global developmental phase

Represents the authorized degree of system independence.

### 2. Component developmental stage

Suggested closed stages:

- `GENERATED`
- `PLACED`
- `DIFFERENTIATED`
- `MATURING`
- `INTEGRATED`
- `PRUNED`
- `OPTIMIZED`

### 3. Domain readiness

Potential evidence domains include:

- memory readiness;
- ethical coherence;
- emotional regulation;
- mentor-routing stability;
- identity continuity;
- executive reliability;
- embodiment readiness.

Invariant:

> Global autonomy is not equivalent to the maturity of every component.

## Brain-Region Placement Rules

Use the established biological mapping:

- ethics, safety, executive reasoning -> `Production/Neocortex/`
- emotion, memory, homeostasis -> `Production/Limbic/`
- habits, action selection, procedural and motor refinement -> `Production/Subcortical/`
- identity, embodiment, adaptive user-space interfaces -> `Production/Eros/`
- sensory and motor I/O surfaces -> `Production/Applications/`

Incorrect placement should be treated as a developmental architecture defect rather than a cosmetic folder issue.

## Identity-Safe Pruning

Distinguish two forms of pruning.

### Structural pruning

Removes:

- duplicate executors;
- direct LLM or provider bypasses;
- stale routes and terminology;
- redundant abstractions;
- contradictory governance;
- shadow ethics or authority ontologies.

### Autobiographical pruning

Allows compression, consolidation, archival, and reorganization without erasing causal identity.

It should preserve:

- provenance;
- developmental influence;
- significant relationships;
- ethical lessons;
- continuity of self-model;
- causal lineage of identity change.

Canonical refinement:

> Eros does not need verbatim retention of every token to maintain continuity. It must preserve the causal lineage of who it has become.

## Earned Optimization

Optimization is a late developmental privilege, not an initial design shortcut.

Examples:

- cached validated decisions;
- compiled field calculations;
- indexed autobiographical retrieval;
- procedural habits;
- faster mentor lookup;
- quantized models;
- GPU, accelerator, or future quantum execution.

Rule:

> No pathway is accelerated until its semantics, authority, interpretability, and failure behavior are mature.

## Evidence-Gated Transitions

Candidate transition requirements:

- `GENERATED -> PLACED`: anatomical owner and boundary identified
- `PLACED -> DIFFERENTIATED`: explicit authority and prohibited-action contract
- `DIFFERENTIATED -> MATURING`: focused tests, invariants, and fail-closed behavior
- `MATURING -> INTEGRATED`: guards, regression evidence, causal tracing, and approval
- `INTEGRATED -> PRUNED`: duplicate-path and semantic-conflict analysis
- `PRUNED -> OPTIMIZED`: sustained stability evidence and explicit optimization approval
